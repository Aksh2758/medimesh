## SCENARIO 1 — 🟢 GREEN (Non-Urgent)
**Expected: GREEN · SAFE · ~15s**

**Symptoms field:**
```
Female, 5 years old. Mild fever 37.9°C for 1 day. Runny nose, mild cough.
Alert and playful. Eating and drinking normally. RR 28/min, HR 98 bpm.
No chest indrawing. No fast breathing. No danger signs. No neck stiffness.
```
**Current meds:** none
**Proposed tx:** paracetamol

**What to say in demo:**
> "Viral URTI — the agents correctly identify no danger signs, 
> assign GREEN, and recommend symptomatic care. No referral needed."

---

## SCENARIO 2 — 🟡 YELLOW (Urgent)
**Expected: YELLOW · SAFE · ~15s**

**Symptoms field:**
```
Male, 3 years old. Fever 38.6°C for 3 days. Cough and fast breathing.
RR 44/min, HR 128 bpm, Temp 38.6°C. Alert, reduced appetite but drinking.
No chest indrawing. No neck stiffness. No general danger signs.
```
**Current meds:** none
**Proposed tx:** amoxicillin 250mg three times daily

**What to say in demo:**
> "Fast breathing above the WHO threshold for this age — 
> agents flag pneumonia, assign YELLOW, and validate amoxicillin as safe."

---

## SCENARIO 3 — 🔴 RED + Drug Interaction (Emergency)
**Expected: RED · UNSAFE · ~15s**
*Most impressive demo — use this last*

**Symptoms field:**
```
Male, 8 months old. Watery diarrhoea for 2 days, 10 episodes today.
Lethargic, sunken eyes, not able to drink. Skin pinch returns very slowly.
HR 172 bpm, RR 42/min, Temp 38.1°C. Fontanelle sunken.
```
**Current meds:** digoxin 0.125mg daily
**Proposed tx:** amiodarone, ORS

**What to say in demo:**
> "Severe dehydration — RED immediately. 
> But watch the Drug Audit: amiodarone + digoxin is flagged UNSAFE — 
> the auditor catches a potentially fatal interaction the nurse might have missed."

## DEMO RECORDING TIPS
1. Use the Quick Scenario buttons in the UI — they pre-fill everything
2. Record in 1080p, zoom in on the triage card when it appears
3. Pause on the drug interaction flag for Scenario 3 — that's your wow moment
4. Show the WHO evidence expander briefly — proves grounding
5. Target: 90 seconds total. Script: 15s intro → 3 demos (20s each) → 15s close
